# Risk of Cardiovascular and Degenerative Tissue Disease Induced by Space Radiation

## Deep Space Galactic Cosmic Ray Exposure
Beyond low Earth orbit, astronauts face continuous high-energy Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCR) and episodic Solar Particle Events (SPE). Unlike terrestrial photon radiation, high-Z and energy (HZE) nuclei cause dense ionization tracks along biological tissue, triggering oxidative stress, DNA double-strand breaks, and persistent microvascular endothelial inflammation.

## Cardiovascular Biomarkers and Clinical Surveillance
Biomarkers of radiation-induced endothelial dysfunction include elevated circulating endothelin-1, inflammatory cytokines (IL-6, TNF-alpha), and oxidative plasma peroxides. Space radiation interacts synergistically with microgravity unloading to accelerate vascular stiffness and alter arterial baroreflex sensitivity.
